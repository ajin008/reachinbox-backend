Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):
Q: Is my showroom data safe?
A: Yes. Each showroom has its own private database. Your data is never mixed with another dealer's. Documents are stored privately, and everything is backed up daily.

Q: Can my staff use Odoline on their phones?
A: Yes. Odoline works in any phone or computer browser and can be installed like an app on mobile devices.

Q: Can my business partners see the accounts without changing things by mistake?
A: Yes. Up to 3 partners get their own read-only login in addition to the primary owner (4 owner logins total), and every action is logged.

Q: What happens if I stop paying?
A: Your data is never deleted. Your account is paused until you renew.
